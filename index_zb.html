Privacy Policy

Zen Bricks: Wind Chime Break
Last updated: 28 October 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Mindspark Digital ("we", "us") handles information in the Zen Bricks: Wind Chime Break app ("the App").

1. Information we collect

We do not require you to create an account, and we do not ask for your name, email address, phone number, or precise location.

Game progress (levels, stars, settings, unlocked items) is stored only on your device. We do not receive or store it on our servers.

2. Third-party services

The App uses the following third-party services, which may collect data under their own policies:

  • Google AdMob (Google Mobile Ads SDK): shows ads. It may collect your Advertising ID, IP address (approximate location), device and operating system information, and ad interaction and diagnostic data, in order to serve and measure ads and to prevent fraud.
  • Google Play Billing: processes in-app purchases. Google handles your payment information; we never see your card or UPI details. We receive only purchase confirmation (such as the product ID and purchase token) so we can unlock the items you bought.
  • Google Play services: may collect crash and diagnostic data.

Google's privacy policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

3. How we use information

  • To show ads and keep the App free
  • To process and restore your purchases
  • To keep the App secure and working properly

4. Sharing

We do not sell your personal data. Data is shared only with the Google services listed above, as needed to provide ads and purchases.

5. Ads and your choices

You can reset or delete your Advertising ID, or opt out of ads personalization, in your Android settings (Settings > Privacy > Ads).

6. Data retention and deletion

Because your progress is stored only on your device, you can delete it at any time by clearing the App's data or uninstalling the App. Data held by Google is governed by Google's policies.

7. Children's privacy

The App is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them.

8. Security

We do not transmit your game data to our servers. Data handled by Google services is protected by Google's security practices.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time and will change the "Last updated" date above when we do.
